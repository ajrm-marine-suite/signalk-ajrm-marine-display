AJRM Marine Display Help
Simulator/test release: AJRM Marine Display is an aid to awareness, not a navigation or collision-avoidance authority. Use the chart, AIS targets, and alerts as supporting information only. Head-on, Give Way, Stand On, overtaking, and other avoidance prompts are limited indications based on available AIS, heading, CPA, and TCPA data; they are not advice or a determination under the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea (COLREGs). AJRM Marine may not know essential facts such as whether your vessel is sailing or motoring, whether vessels are in sight, the other vessel's operational status, visibility, sea room, or local constraints. The skipper remains responsible for assessing the complete situation, applying the COLREGs, and deciding and carrying out safe action.

Display is deliberately lean: it draws the chart, shows targets, presents active alerts, and offers the controls needed while sailing. AJRM Marine Traffic, Notifications, Audio, Capture, Snapshot, and Voyage Viewer own their deeper settings and diagnostics. The former standalone Logger is retired; Capture now owns recording and replay.

Map controls

Hover a map icon, or move keyboard focus to it, to see its purpose. The same tooltip convention is used by Display, DR the other suite map views and Locations.

  • Centre vessel: resumes following, with configurable chart space ahead along COG.
  • Chart selector: chooses basemaps, Signal K chart layers, Auto Charts, OpenSeaMap, and harbour limits.
  • Cycle chart: cycles enabled charts overlapping the map centre, shows the basemap alone, then returns to Automatic selection.
  • AIS Targets: opens the live target list and selected-vessel details.
  • Profiles: selects the active AJRM Marine Traffic profile, adjusts current-profile sensitivity, and provides Drop Anchor/Un-anchor chart marking. Drop Anchor records the current position and depth below keel; Un-anchor selects Coastal.
  • Settings: controls Display-only options such as alerts, dark mode, no-sleep, own-vessel icon, saved location symbols, the tide-status panel, harbour limits, and TCPA guide lines.
  • Help: opens this quick Display reference.

Routes opens, imports, reverses, saves, and closes GPX/Signal K routes. Only show routes crossing the current chart area narrows Pi, Signal K and selected device routes to those with a visible waypoint or a route leg crossing the screen. On this device, choose several GPX files or an entire route folder first. The lists then update locally as the chart is moved. Browser security requires local files or the folder to be selected again after a page reload. Voyage observation records a timestamped skipper note in the active Capture voyage and can attach Snapshot evidence when that service is available.

Tides and locations shows optional anchorage, mooring and other saved-location symbols. Its popup consumes Tidal Database's shared resolver to show current height, trend, next high/low waters, datum, station, selection reason, source freshness and the tide curve. It also gives an astronomical spring-neap estimate, including days after the preceding phase and before the next; local tidal response may lag that lunar phase. The graph range defaults to seven days and is remembered by this browser. Automatic tidal-port selection uses the live vessel position when available and otherwise the chart centre; a selected standard or secondary port overrides it until automatic selection is restored.

Assisted anchoring asks before selecting the Anchored profile when the shared location service finds the vessel stationary at an anchorage or mooring. Confirming does not create an anchor mark. Use Drop Anchor at the actual drop point to record its position and depth. Vessel Traffic releases Anchored only after sustained evidence: by default, more than 2 knots for 60 seconds or, when a manual anchor mark exists, more than 100 m from it for 30 seconds.

Alerts shown by Display

Alert severity, CPA/TCPA, profile choice, muting, and speech decisions come from the suite services. Display shows those projections; it does not recalculate traffic safety state.

  • Bottom alert panel: keeps active alerts visible at the bottom of the chart.
  • Popup alerts: shows a modal popup for important alert events.
  • Popup alert sound: plays a short local browser sound for popups when suite audio is not muted.
Charts and following

Auto Charts chooses a chart for the current vessel or map position. When charts overlap, use Cycle chart or press the configured key (C by default) to select the next chart. A manual selection remains through zoom and overzoom, then returns to Automatic after the last chart or when the map centre leaves its coverage. Manual panning pauses following; press centre vessel to follow own vessel again. By default, following keeps 66% of the visible chart ahead along COG and 34% behind; adjust this under Settings > Own-vessel appearance. Without COG the vessel is centred. OpenSeaMap is a transparent seamark overlay and is kept above chart tiles. Expand Chart folders beneath Auto Charts to enable or disable complete Charts Provider Simple directory trees; nested folders inherit a disabled parent.

Vessels and routes

Click own vessel for its navigation, GPS, route, and system status; click another target for its AIS and encounter details. Recognised SAR-aircraft MMSIs remain visible with an aircraft symbol but are not treated as surface-vessel collision candidates. A displayed route is a visual reference only unless another explicitly configured Signal K service activates it.

After an update

If the version changed but the browser still behaves like an older release, hard-refresh the page. On Safari, try Option+Cmd+R on Mac or clear website data for the Signal K hostname on iPhone/iPad.
